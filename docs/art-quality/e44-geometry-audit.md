# E4.4 character geometry and art audit

The Project Owner approved E4.3 engineering and the Courier technical pilot, selected B1 M2P as the Surveyor comparison baseline and authorized these geometry revisions. Both characters keep their 64 × 64 canvas and single three-quarter-right view. [Offline comparison](../LumaSprite-Engine-E4.4-Report.html)

| Root cause | Geometry change | Native-scale result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Surveyor's forearm rose only 2 px across 13 px from elbow to wrist. The sleeve read as a horizontal bar across the torso. | R1 moves the near elbow from (32,34) to (32,36) and regenerates the upper-arm and forearm masks. All other landmark and part-mask coordinates match B1. | The near sleeve bends down before the forearm rises to the wrist. The opening between arm and torso narrows from four rows to two and meets the original minimum. Grip contact remains seven pixels. The handle keeps two open rows and the lamp body stays clear. |
| The wide cap brim hid the forehead. The shallow shoulder drop and forward knee bulges reinforced a side-on crouch. | R2 inherits R1 and shortens the far brim by 2 px, opens the cheek silhouette, moves the coat plane boundary, increases the shoulder drop from 3 to 4 px and straightens the knee chains. The head extent stays 14 px wide with 3 px headroom. | More face is visible under the cap and the knees read straighter. The right-facing profile remains pronounced on some backgrounds. The near knee offset drops from 2 to 1 px and the far from 2.88 to 0.88 px. Both boot soles, the nine open leg-gap rows and the Surveyor pivot (30,59) carry through. |
| Courier's far hand blended into the coat. The high, broad satchel and strap colored a large area of the torso as one mass. The hood hid the near cheek and the far knee bent forward. | The single v3 revision opens the hood and cheek, shifts the coat plane, moves the arms, lowers and narrows the pouch and adds a structural flap seam. The far knee moves one pixel back. | Visible far-hand coverage increases from 3 to 18 pixels. The pouch becomes smaller at 68 visible pixels versus 76 and its flap uses an existing leather shadow. Two planted soles, six open leg-gap rows and pivot (32,58) carry through. The leather and russet coat are close in value in the dark preview. |

Surveyor R1 and R2 use the same M2P ramps, top-left lighting and mask-based material routing as B1. The glass retains 10 bright warm pixels. Courier keeps its E4.3 M2 ramps. All three new alphas and their own part masks are versioned with lineage pinned to the parent lock. [Revision comparison](../../benchmarks/results/e44/revision-comparison.json)

R1 isolates the arm change. R2 is the full geometry revision to compare with B1 at 1×. View the cap in Outdoor and Dungeon, then check the sleeve clearance and grip on the stocked path.
