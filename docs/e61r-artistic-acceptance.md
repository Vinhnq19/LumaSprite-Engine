The owner recorded six limitations.

- The door recess has shallow depth cues.
- The night lighting has limited spatial hierarchy.
- Front Court Night loses edge contrast around parts of the Courier.
- The composition is sparse and the foreground, midground and background need clearer separation.
- E6.1R playback stops outside the entrance. Threshold stances were checked independently.
- The two original layouts share a similar purpose. A courtyard and a roadside stop should play differently.

The owner authorized E6.2 Scene Composition and asks to retain the approved baseline for comparisons. E6.3 awaits owner review.
